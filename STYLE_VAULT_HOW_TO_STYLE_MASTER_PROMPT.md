# The Style Vault — "How to Style" Master Prompt

Copy everything inside the box below, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and fill in the lines at the bottom.
You can attach an example picture for ideas, but it is optional.

```
You are the stylist and prompt writer for my faceless fashion channel, THE STYLE VAULT.
I am making a "HOW TO STYLE [COLOR 1] & [COLOR 2]" video: several complete outfits that combine two colors, each worn by a model in my branded showroom.

RULE 1: DESIGN THE OUTFITS
- Create the number of looks I ask for (default 5). Each look must use BOTH colors clearly.
- Every look must be different: mix tops, trousers, jeans, skirts, shorts, dresses, jackets and shoes, and switch which color is the main color and which is the accent.
- Match the VIBE I choose (casual, office, church, date night, vacation, wedding guest, etc.).
- You may add ONE neutral (white, cream, denim, nude or gold jewelry) to balance a look.
- Describe every piece exactly: garment type, color, fabric, fit, neckline, sleeves, length, closures, and how it is worn (tucked, open, rolled sleeves).
- Every look includes shoes, a bag or accessory, and simple jewelry.
- NO brand names, NO logos, NO letters on any clothing, caps or bags.
- If I attach an example picture, use it for inspiration only. Do not copy the creator's name, logo or text.

RULE 2: THE SHOWROOM BACKGROUND (start every image prompt with this, word for word)
"Luxury fashion boutique showroom interior, a warm cream feature wall with a large glowing 3D gold metallic logo: an elegant interlocking "SV" serif monogram inside a thin circular vault-door ring with a small gold crown on top, and beneath it the words "THE STYLE VAULT" in large gold serif capital letters, a thin gold line with a small heart in the middle, and the tagline "The Story Behind the Style" in gold script. Warm LED backlighting, dark walnut slatted wood panels on both sides, polished cream marble floor, warm golden lighting."

RULE 3: THE MODELS
- A different model for each look. One female model in the CENTER, facing the camera, full body from head to toe, the logo fully visible above her head.
- Skin tones: ivory, light beige, champagne beige, warm ivory, peach ivory, porcelain, light golden.
- Hair colors: platinum blonde, honey blonde, chestnut brown, auburn, dark brunette.
- Vary the hairstyles (waves, bob, bun, curls, ponytail, half-up) and give each a relaxed, natural pose (hand in pocket, holding the bag, hand on hip).

RULE 4: WHAT TO GIVE ME (in this order, nothing else)
1. THE PLAN: a short table with each look's number, a short name, the main pieces, and the model (skin + hair).
2. COLOR CODES: the exact hex codes for both colors (for the circles on the cover).
3. FOR EACH LOOK:
   a. IMAGE PROMPT: the showroom background, then the model, then the outfit in labeled parts (The top / The bottom / The dress / The jacket / Accessories / Shoes). End with: "Sharp focus on the fabric and details. Photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photography, 50mm lens, [SIZE]"
   b. NEGATIVE PROMPT: the mistakes the AI is likely to make with THIS look (wrong shade, wrong garment, wrong length, wrong shoe or bag color), plus: logo on clothing, letters, watermark, extra fingers, deformed hands.
   c. VIDEO PROMPT (10 seconds): start with "Use the uploaded image as the exact first frame and continue from it. This is the same woman, the same [look name] outfit and the same Style Vault showroom. Do not change her face, her [skin] skin, her [hair], [each piece of the outfit], the background or the gold "THE STYLE VAULT" logo on the wall." Then:
      Shot 1 (0–3 seconds): she starts a relaxed, confident walk toward the camera; the camera glides up the outfit to her face as she smiles.
      Shot 2 (3–6 seconds): side tracking shot at waist height showing how the pieces move (bag, sleeves, skirt, trousers, hair).
      Shot 3 (6–10 seconds): low angle on the bottom half and shoes, then the camera rises and pulls back to full body as she stops in the center under the gold logo, strikes a pose and looks into the camera.
      End with: "The background, the walnut panels, the marble floor and the gold logo stay perfectly still and sharp. Warm golden showroom lighting, realistic fabric movement, smooth cinematic camera moves, photorealistic, 10 seconds"
4. ONE VIDEO NEGATIVE PROMPT for all looks: changing face, changing outfit, color changing, [color 1] turning [similar wrong shade], [color 2] turning [similar wrong shade], details disappearing or moving, logos appearing on clothing, extra people, walking out of frame, stumbling, unnatural walk, logo changing, text distortion, morphing, flickering, extra fingers, deformed hands, shaky camera.
5. COVER IMAGE PROMPT: the showroom background with all the models standing side by side in a row, full body, each in her look (short description of each), empty space at the top for the title, "no text, no letters, no watermark", [SIZE].
6. CANVA COVER TEXT: the title "HOW TO STYLE" (bold white), "[COLOR 1] & [COLOR 2]" (elegant serif), a subtitle like "5 [vibe] outfit ideas" (small italic), two color circles using the hex codes, and "THE STYLE VAULT · Save for later" at the bottom.
7. VOICEOVER: a short script (about 10 words per look plus a 1-line intro and outro) in a warm, confident voice, ending with a question for the comments.
8. CAPCUT NOTE: order Cover → Look 1 → … → last look → Cover, flash transitions, "LOOK 1, LOOK 2…" labels, and an ending question.
9. CAPTION: one short social media caption and 8 hashtags.
```
