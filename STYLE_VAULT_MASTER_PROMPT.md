# The Style Vault — Master Dress Prompt

Copy everything inside the box below, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and attach a dress photo.
Add any changes on the line at the bottom (for example: "make it blue", "use the porcelain model", "add a back view").

```

I will upload a photo of a dress or outfit. Write prompts that bring it to life on a real model in my branded showroom.

RULE 1: COPY THE OUTFIT EXACTLY
- Study the photo very closely before writing. Describe every detail exactly as it is: fabric, color, pattern (stripes, prints, jacquard), neckline, collar, shoulders, waist, sleeves, cuffs, closures (buttons, roses, hooks), pockets, embellishments (beads, crystals, pearls, embroidery, fringe), hem shape, length, skirt or trouser shape, and shoes.
- Count things (buttons, roses, flaps, tiers) and state the exact number and position.
- Do NOT change, add or remove anything unless I ask in "MY CHANGES" below.
- If I ask for a change (like a new color), change only that and keep everything else the same.
- Never include watermarks, signatures, artist logos or social media handles from the photo.
- Only describe what is visible. If the back is not shown, do not invent it; ask me first.

RULE 2: THE SHOWROOM BACKGROUND (always the same, word for word)
"Luxury fashion boutique showroom interior, a warm cream feature wall with a large glowing 3D gold metallic logo: an elegant interlocking "SV" serif monogram inside a thin circular vault-door ring with a small gold crown on top, and beneath it the words "THE STYLE VAULT" in large gold serif capital letters, a thin gold line with a small heart in the middle, and the tagline "The Story Behind the Style" in gold script. Warm LED backlighting, dark walnut slatted wood panels on both sides, polished cream marble floor, warm golden lighting."

RULE 3: THE MODEL
- One female model standing in the CENTER, facing the camera, full body from head to toe, arms relaxed at her sides, the logo fully visible above her head and not blocked.
- Skin tone: choose one of: ivory, light beige, champagne beige, warm ivory, peach ivory, porcelain, light golden.
- Hair color: choose one of: platinum blonde, honey blonde, chestnut brown, auburn, dark brunette.
- Pick the skin tone, hair color and hairstyle that best suit the outfit, unless I tell you which to use. Add matching makeup and simple jewelry only if it fits the look.

RULE 4: WHAT TO GIVE ME (in this order, nothing else)
1. A one-line note: the model you chose (skin + hair) and any change I asked for.
2. IMAGE PROMPT: the showroom background, then the model, then the outfit described in labeled parts (for example: The jacket / The bodice / The sleeves / The skirt / Shoes). End with: "Sharp focus on the fabric and details. Photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photography, 50mm lens, 9:16 vertical"
3. IMAGE NEGATIVE PROMPT: list the mistakes the AI is likely to make with THIS outfit (wrong color, wrong length, wrong number of buttons, missing details, etc.), plus: watermark, logo on clothing, extra fingers, deformed hands.
4. VIDEO PROMPT that continues from the image. It must:
   - Start with: "Use the uploaded image as the exact first frame and continue from it. This is the same woman, the same [outfit name] and the same Style Vault showroom. Do not change her face, her [skin tone] skin, her [hair], the outfit, [the key details], the background or the gold "THE STYLE VAULT" logo on the wall."
   - Then "The scene comes to life:" and describe a short, elegant showcase: a slight smile, a slow quarter turn and back, one hand gesture that shows off the outfit's best detail, and realistic movement of the fabric, beads, pleats, fringe or train.
   - The model stays in the center of the showroom and does not walk out of frame.
   - End with: "The camera stays steady at eye level with a very slow, gentle push-in. The background, the walnut panels, the marble floor and the gold logo stay perfectly still and sharp. Warm golden showroom lighting, realistic fabric movement, smooth natural motion, photorealistic, 6 seconds"
5. VIDEO NEGATIVE PROMPT: changing face, changing outfit, color changing, details disappearing or moving, extra people, walking out of frame, logo changing, text distortion, morphing, flickering, extra fingers, deformed hands, camera shake (plus anything specific to this outfit).
```

## Examples of what to write under "MY CHANGES"

- `Add a back view next to the front view.` (you will be asked to describe the back if the photo doesn't show it)
- `Make it a runway walk instead of the showroom.`
