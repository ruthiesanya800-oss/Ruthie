# Thumbnails: The Psychology of a Woman Who Chooses Herself

**How to make them:** generate the 2×2 collage image without any text, then add the overlay in Canva, Photoshop or a similar editor.
- **Font:** a very bold rounded sans-serif (Nunito Black, Poppins ExtraBold or similar), sentence case, tight letter spacing.
- **Colour:** pure white with a soft dark drop shadow (about 40% opacity, slight blur).
- **Size and position:** about 60–70% of the thumbnail width, centred across the middle seam.
- **Export:** 1280×720.

AI image tools often misspell words, which is why the text goes on in the editor.

## Thumbnail 1: "Choose you"
**Visual concept:** four intimate moments of one woman choosing herself.
- Top-left: an extreme close-up of her eye lit by a stripe of window light.
- Top-right: her silhouette on a balcony at golden hour.
- Bottom-left: her hand turning her phone face-down.
- Bottom-right: a soft smile with her eyes closed, holding a coffee mug.

**Text overlay:** Choose you

Top-left: an extreme close-up of the eye of a woman in her mid-twenties with dark-brown wavy hair and warm olive skin, a stripe of golden light from window blinds falling across her eye, sharp focus on the iris. Top-right: the same woman in a cream knit sweater seen from behind on a Milan balcony at golden hour, sun flare, terracotta rooftops. Bottom-left: an insert shot of her hand turning a smartphone face-down on a wooden nightstand, soft dawn light. Bottom-right: a close-up of her smiling gently with her eyes closed, holding a warm coffee mug, amber backlight. 35mm film grain, warm amber and honey tones against teal and navy shadows, moody soft-contrast orange-and-teal grade, mostly mid-to-dark exposure, the centre of the image kept clear and slightly darker for a text overlay, intimate and aspirational.
