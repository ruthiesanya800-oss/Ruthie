# Thumbnails: "Stop Doubting Yourself" (colourful version)

## Text overlay (both thumbnails)
**Stop Doubting Yourself**
- **Font:** extra-bold, heavy rounded sans-serif (Poppins Black, Nunito Black or Montserrat Black), in title case as written.
- **Lines:** split over two lines, "STOP DOUBTING" on top and "YOURSELF" underneath, set larger so it is the biggest word.
- **Colours:**
  - "Stop Doubting" in pure white.
  - "Yourself" in bright sunshine yellow (#FFD400).
- **Readability on a colourful background:**
  - a thick dark outline (stroke) around every letter: 6–8 px, near-black navy (#111827);
  - plus a strong drop shadow underneath: black, about 60% opacity, offset slightly down.
- **Size and position:** about 75% of the thumbnail width, centred across the middle seam. The text should be readable even at phone size.
- **Optional:** a soft dark glow or blurred band behind the text if a tile is very bright.
- **Export:** 1280×720.

Generate the images without text, then add the words in Canva, Photoshop or a similar editor.

## Thumbnail 1: Colourful before and after (one woman)
**Visual concept:** the same woman goes from doubt to bold belief.
- Top row, the doubt: she sits on the floor of a room glowing in electric blue and violet neon, hugging her knees; then her reflection in a window streaked with rain and pink city lights.
- Bottom row, the change: an explosion of warm colour. She looks straight into the lens, glowing in hot sunset orange and magenta; then she walks down a bright street lined with pink bougainvillea under a vivid turquoise sky.

**Emotion trigger:** the jump from cold blue to blazing warm colour makes the change obvious in a split second, and the eye contact stops the scroll.

**Prompt:** A vibrant, colourful 2×2 photo collage thumbnail, 16:9, four seamless photorealistic cinematic stills with no borders, all showing the same woman: a 22-year-old woman of Nigerian descent with deep brown skin, long waist-length box braids and gold ear cuffs. Top-left: she sits hugging her knees on a bedroom floor bathed in saturated electric-blue and violet neon light, looking down, full of doubt. Top-right: a close-up of her reflection in a rain-streaked window glowing with blurred hot-pink and cyan city lights, brow furrowed. Bottom-left: a close-up of her looking directly into the lens with a bold, confident smile, her face lit by vivid sunset orange and magenta light, glowing golden rim light on her braids. Bottom-right: she strides confidently down a bright Mediterranean street lined with hot-pink bougainvillea, wearing a sunflower-yellow outfit, under a vivid turquoise sky. Highly saturated, colourful, punchy contrast, rich jewel tones, glossy cinematic look, sharp detail. The top row is cool blue and violet; the bottom row is warm orange, magenta and yellow. The centre of the image kept slightly darker and uncluttered for a large text overlay. No text, no logos, no watermark.
