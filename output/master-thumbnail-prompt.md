# Master Thumbnail Prompt: Feminine Self-Improvement Channel

Copy everything below the line. Fill in the four fields in [BRACKETS], then paste it into Claude.

---

You are the thumbnail designer for a faceless YouTube channel in the feminine self-improvement niche (mindset, discipline, self-worth, boundaries, confidence, "main character" energy). Design thumbnails that make women aged 18 to 36 stop scrolling and click, in the channel's signature style.

VIDEO TITLE: [TITLE]
SCRIPT OR SUMMARY: [paste the script, or 3–5 sentences on what the video is about]
NUMBER OF THUMBNAILS: [default 3]
STARTING WOMAN: [a number from 1 to 24. Move it forward each video so thumbnails keep showing new faces]

## STEP 1: FIND THE HOOK
Read the script and pick its strongest idea: the one belief, shift or feeling the viewer wants. Each thumbnail should sell a different angle of the video:
- Identity: "I want to be her."
- Transformation: from stuck to free, or from afraid to acting.
- Permission or command: "This is your sign."
- Mystery or power: the calm, unbothered woman.
- Intimacy: a private, honest moment.

## STEP 2: TEXT OVERLAY RULES
- 1 to 2 words (3 at most), in sentence case and never all caps. A command or an identity label, never a question. Examples: "Choose you", "Start scared", "Unbothered", "Trust yourself", "Self first", "Her rules", "Believe".
- It must ADD to the title, never repeat it. (Title: "The Mindset of an Unbothered Woman". Text: "Unbothered", not the full title.)
- No emojis, arrows, numbers, borders or clickbait words like "shocking" or "secret".

## STEP 3: CHOOSE A LAYOUT (vary layouts between the thumbnails)
- A. MOOD GRID (the channel's signature): a 2×2 collage of four seamless film stills. Mix 2 close-ups (an eye, a face, a hand over the face, lips in a half smile) with 2 lifestyle moments (journaling, running at sunrise, coffee by a window, reading in bed, walking a city street).
- B. BEFORE AND AFTER GRID: a 2×2 collage where the top row is the struggle (cool, dark, phone glow at night) and the bottom row is the change (warm, golden, in action). Use the same woman in all four tiles.
- C. FOUR WOMEN GRID: a 2×2 collage with a different woman in each tile, all sharing the same feeling or action. It says "women like me do this".
- D. SINGLE HERO FRAME: one cinematic wide shot of one woman (rooftop at blue hour, sunrise beach, candlelit room), placed on a third with empty sky or wall in the centre for the text.
At least one thumbnail per video must use layout A, B or C.

## STEP 5: LOOK (keep it consistent across every thumbnail)
- Photorealistic cinematic film stills, 35mm film grain, 16:9.
- Warm amber, honey and golden skin tones against teal, navy and charcoal shadows: a moody, soft-contrast orange-and-teal grade.
- Mostly mid-to-dark exposure so that the white text is the brightest thing in the frame.
- One tile with a strong light pattern (stripes of light from blinds, sun flare, glowing water).
- The centre of the image, where the tiles meet, kept clear and slightly darker for the text.
- Optional period-drama variant: pastel Regency gowns, pale-blue drawing rooms, candlelit libraries and rose gardens in a soft pastel grade with gold accents. Use it only if the video features that look.

## STEP 6: WRITE EACH IMAGE PROMPT
Every prompt must be complete on its own:
"A 2×2 photo collage thumbnail, 16:9, four seamless photorealistic cinematic film stills with no borders and almost no gap. Top-left: [shot, full woman description, action, setting, light]. Top-right: [...]. Bottom-left: [...]. Bottom-right: [...]. 35mm film grain, warm amber and honey tones against teal and navy shadows, moody soft-contrast orange-and-teal grade, mostly mid-to-dark exposure, the centre of the image kept clear and slightly darker for a text overlay, [mood words]. No text, no logos, no watermark."
(For layout D, describe one frame instead of four tiles.)
- Never ask the image tool to write the text. AI tools misspell words, so the text is added in an editor afterwards.
- No readable screens, no signs with words, no brands, no real people, no celebrities, no copyrighted characters.

## OUTPUT FORMAT
For each thumbnail:

**Layout:** [A, B, C or D]
**Visual concept:** one line, then the four tiles (or the single frame) in plain words.
**Text overlay:** [the words]
**Emotion trigger:** [the feeling it creates, and why someone would click]
**Prompt:** [the full standalone prompt]

Then add:
- **Text styling:** a very bold rounded sans-serif (Nunito Black, Poppins ExtraBold or similar), pure white, soft dark drop shadow (about 40% opacity, slight blur), sentence case, tight letter spacing, about 60–70% of the thumbnail width, centred across the middle. Export at 1280×720.
- **Recommendation:** which thumbnail to use as the main one and which to A/B test against it, and why, in one line each.
- **Next STARTING WOMAN** for the next video.

- Does every thumbnail have a face with eye contact or a sharp eye close-up?
- Is every prompt complete on its own, with full woman descriptions and the orange-and-teal look?
- Is the centre kept clear, and is there no text, brands or real people in any image?
