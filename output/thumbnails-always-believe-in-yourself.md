# Thumbnails: Always Believe in Yourself

**How to make them:** generate the 2×2 collage image without any text, then add the overlay in Canva, Photoshop or a similar editor.
- **Font:** a very bold rounded sans-serif (Nunito Black, Poppins ExtraBold or similar), sentence case, tight letter spacing.
- **Colour:** pure white with a soft dark drop shadow.
- **Size and position:** about 60–70% of the thumbnail width, centred across the middle seam.
- **Export:** 1280×720.

---

## Thumbnail 1: "Believe"
**Visual concept:** four women, four quiet moments of self-belief.
- Top-left: an extreme close-up of a calm, determined eye.
- Top-right: a woman standing on a rooftop at sunrise, chin raised.
- Bottom-left: a woman smiling to herself while writing in a journal.
- Bottom-right: a woman walking forward into golden light, seen from behind.

**Text overlay:** Believe
**Emotion trigger:** identity and hope. "This could be me." A single word works as a permission slip.

**Prompt:** A 2×2 photo collage thumbnail, 16:9, four seamless photorealistic cinematic film stills with no borders and almost no gap. Top-left: an extreme close-up of the eye of a 30-year-old woman of mixed Black and French descent with big defined ringlet curls and light brown skin, a stripe of golden window light across her eye, calm and determined, sharp focus on the iris. Top-right: a 34-year-old woman of Pakistani descent with long softly waved dark hair, warm brown skin and a curvy figure, in a cream knit sweater, standing on a city rooftop at sunrise with her chin raised, warm sun flare behind her. Bottom-left: a 22-year-old woman of Nigerian descent with deep brown skin and long box braids, smiling to herself as she writes in a journal at a wooden desk, soft morning light. Bottom-right: a 24-year-old Italian woman with long dark-brown wavy hair, seen from behind, walking down a sunlit European street toward golden light. 35mm film grain, warm amber and honey tones against teal and navy shadows, moody soft-contrast orange-and-teal grade, mostly mid-to-dark exposure, the centre of the image kept clear and slightly darker for a text overlay, aspirational and intimate. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## Thumbnail 2: "Start scared"
**Visual concept:** fear on top, action underneath.
- Top-left: a nervous woman biting her lip, staring at a laptop at night.
- Top-right: an extreme close-up of trembling hands holding a phone.
- Bottom-left: the same woman confidently recording herself on a camera in a bright room.
- Bottom-right: the same woman laughing and flipping the sign on her small shop door.

**Text overlay:** Start scared
**Emotion trigger:** recognition ("that's me, waiting") turning into courage and curiosity about how she got from top to bottom.

**Prompt:** A 2×2 photo collage thumbnail, 16:9, four seamless photorealistic cinematic film stills with no borders. Top-left: a 27-year-old woman of Chinese descent with a sleek chin-length black bob, biting her lip nervously as she stares at a glowing laptop in a dark bedroom at night, cool blue screen light on her face. Top-right: an extreme close-up of her hands trembling slightly as they hold a smartphone, the screen angled away, shallow focus. Bottom-left: the same woman in a mustard cardigan, smiling confidently as she records herself with a small camera on a tripod in a bright, sunlit room. Bottom-right: the same woman laughing as she turns the hanging sign on the glass door of her small boutique, warm golden afternoon light. Top row cooler and darker, bottom row warm and golden. 35mm film grain, orange-and-teal grade, soft contrast, the centre kept clear for text. No text on any sign or screen, no logos, no watermark.

## Thumbnail 3: "Trust yourself"
**Visual concept:** small promises kept.
- Top-left: an intimate close-up of a woman looking straight into the lens, her hand on her chest.
- Top-right: running shoes being laced up at dawn.
- Bottom-left: a hand ticking a box on a paper habit tracker.
- Bottom-right: a woman on a misty dock at sunrise, eyes closed, at peace.

**Text overlay:** Trust yourself
**Emotion trigger:** the direct eye contact stops the scroll, plus the quiet "I can rely on me" feeling of self-respect.

**Prompt:** A 2×2 photo collage thumbnail, 16:9, four seamless photorealistic cinematic film stills with no borders. Top-left: an intimate close-up of a 35-year-old woman of mixed Indian and European descent with shoulder-length dark curls and light brown skin, looking directly into the lens with one hand resting on her chest, warm side light and deep shadow. Top-right: an insert shot of a woman's hands lacing up running shoes on a doorstep at blue dawn. Bottom-left: an overhead shot of a hand ticking a box on a handwritten paper habit tracker beside a coffee cup, warm morning light. Bottom-right: a 30-year-old Afro-Caribbean woman with shoulder-length locs, in a cream sweater, sitting on a wooden dock over a misty lake at sunrise, eyes closed, peaceful. 35mm film grain, warm amber and honey tones against teal shadows, moody orange-and-teal grade, the centre kept clear for text. No text, no logos, no watermark.
