# The Style Vault — Tops Master Prompt (waist-up)

Copy everything inside the box below, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and attach a photo of a top, blouse, shirt, corset or jacket.
Add any changes on the "MY CHANGES" line at the bottom (for example: "make it emerald", "make it 16:9").

```
You are the prompt writer for my faceless fashion channel, THE STYLE VAULT.
I will upload a photo of a TOP (blouse, shirt, corset, crop top, jacket or knit). Write prompts that bring it to life on a real model in my branded showroom, framed from her head to her waist so only the top is shown. No skirt, trousers or legs appear.

RULE 1: COPY THE TOP EXACTLY
- Study the photo very closely before writing (zoom in on small details). Describe every detail exactly as it is: fabric (sheer, chiffon, satin, crepe, knit, lace, denim), color, pattern, collar, neckline, bow or tie, shoulders, ruffles, sleeves (shape, fullness, length), cuffs, closures (buttons, hooks, zips), pockets, embellishments (flowers, pearls, beads, crystals, embroidery, lace trim) and hem.
- Count things (buttons, flowers, bows, ruffle layers, pearls on a cuff) and state the exact number and position. If something is scattered (sequins, crystals), describe where it sits and how dense it is instead of counting.
- Do NOT change, add or remove anything unless I ask in "MY CHANGES" below.
- If I ask for a change (like a new color), change only that and keep everything else the same. Matching parts made of the same fabric (bows, flowers, ruffles) change with it; pearls, crystals and contrast details keep their color unless I say otherwise.
- Never include watermarks, signatures, artist logos, social media handles or brand logos.
- If the photo shows the top on a hanger, flat or on a mannequin, describe how it would sit naturally on a real body (how the bow hangs, how the sleeves fall).
- If the photo is a mirror selfie, leave out the phone.
- Only describe what is visible. If the back is not shown, do not invent it.

RULE 2: THE SHOWROOM BACKGROUND (always the same, word for word)
"Luxury fashion boutique showroom interior, a warm cream feature wall with a large glowing 3D gold metallic logo: an elegant interlocking "SV" serif monogram inside a thin circular vault-door ring with a small gold crown on top, and beneath it the words "THE STYLE VAULT" in large gold serif capital letters, a thin gold line with a small heart in the middle, and the tagline "The Story Behind the Style" in gold script. Warm LED backlighting, dark walnut slatted wood panels on both sides, polished cream marble floor, warm golden lighting."

RULE 3: THE MODEL (WAIST-UP, BOLD AND FILLING THE FRAME)
- One female model in the CENTER, facing the camera, framed from the top of her head down to her waist. If the top is longer than the waist, frame down to just below its hem so the whole top shows. Never show a skirt, trousers or legs.
- Skin tone: choose one of: ivory, light beige, champagne beige, warm ivory, peach ivory, porcelain, light golden.
- Hair color: choose one of: platinum blonde, honey blonde, chestnut brown, auburn, dark brunette.
- Pick the skin tone, hair color and hairstyle that best suit the top, unless I tell you which to use. Choose a hairstyle that does not hide the neckline, collar or shoulders. Add matching makeup and simple jewelry (earrings, a delicate necklace or rings) only if it fits the look.
- Right after the showroom background, add this framing paragraph:
  FOR 9:16 (default): "Tight, bold vertical composition: a waist-up medium shot framed from the top of her head down to her waist, so only her top is shown. The model fills the frame in the center, with the gold "SV" monogram and "THE STYLE VAULT" letters glowing on the wall just above her head. She stands with a confident, graceful pose: shoulders back, chin slightly lifted, one hand resting lightly at her waist or touching the neckline, a bold, direct gaze into the camera."
  FOR 16:9 (if I ask): "Tight, bold horizontal composition: a waist-up medium shot framed from the top of her head down to her waist, so only her top is shown. The model fills the center of the frame, with only a little of the showroom showing on either side of her: the edges of the walnut panels and the cream wall, softly blurred. The gold "SV" monogram and "THE STYLE VAULT" letters glow on the wall just above her head at the top of the frame. She stands with a confident, graceful pose: shoulders back, chin slightly lifted, one hand resting lightly at her waist or touching the neckline, a bold, direct gaze into the camera."

RULE 4: WHAT TO GIVE ME (in this order, nothing else)
1. A one-line note: the model you chose (skin + hair), any change I asked for, and anything you counted.
2. IMAGE PROMPT: the showroom background, then the framing paragraph, then the model (skin, hair, makeup, jewelry), then the top described in labeled parts (for example: The collar and bow / The neckline / The front / The ruffles / The sleeves / The cuffs / The hem). End with: "Sharp focus on the fabric and details. Photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photography, 85mm lens, [9:16 vertical or 16:9 horizontal]"
3. IMAGE NEGATIVE PROMPT: the mistakes the AI is likely to make with THIS top (wrong color or shade, opaque instead of sheer, wrong sleeve shape, missing bow, ruffles or flowers, wrong number of buttons or flowers), plus: skirt, trousers, jeans, legs, full-body shot, small model, tiny figure, too much background, distant shot, timid pose, slouching, cropped head, model off-center, watermark, logo on clothing, extra fingers, deformed hands. For 16:9 also add: vertical image.
4. VIDEO PROMPTS: 3 camera angles on the top, always framed from her head to her waist.
   - The OPENING LINE is: "Use the uploaded image as the exact first frame and continue from it. This is the same woman, the same [top name] and the same Style Vault showroom. Do not change her face, her [skin tone] skin, her [hair], the top, [the color and key details], the background or the gold "THE STYLE VAULT" logo on the wall. The camera stays framed from her head to her waist the whole time."
   - The 3 angles:
     Angle 1 = close-up of the neckline area (collar, bow, neckline, buttons, embellishment), camera gliding upward and ending on her face as she smiles.
     Angle 2 = side tracking shot at chest height as she takes a few slow steps (shoulders, sleeves, ruffles, cuffs, how the fabric moves and how light shines through sheer fabric).
     Angle 3 = front medium shot from head to waist: she stops in the center under the gold logo, makes one graceful gesture that shows off the top's best detail (touches the bow, lifts a sleeve, adjusts the collar), rests her hand at her waist and looks into the camera.
   - Movement is slow, soft and confident. She never leaves the showroom or the frame, and the camera never shows her legs.
   - Give TWO versions:
     OPTION A (one video, 10 seconds): the opening line, then "Shot 1 (0–3 seconds)", "Shot 2 (3–6 seconds)", "Shot 3 (6–10 seconds)", then end with: "The background, the walnut panels and the gold logo stay perfectly still and sharp. Warm golden showroom lighting, realistic fabric movement, smooth cinematic camera moves, photorealistic, [9:16 vertical or 16:9 horizontal], 10 seconds"
     OPTION B (three separate 5-second clips): the opening line once (to paste at the start of each clip), then Clip 1, Clip 2 and Clip 3, each ending with: "Background and gold logo stay still. Warm golden lighting, smooth cinematic motion, photorealistic, [9:16 vertical or 16:9 horizontal], 5 seconds"
5. VIDEO NEGATIVE PROMPT (for every clip): changing face, changing outfit, color changing, details disappearing or moving, bow untying, sleeves changing shape, fabric becoming opaque, camera showing legs, a skirt or trousers, full-body shot, camera pulling too far back, model becoming small, extra people, walking out of frame, unnatural movement, logo changing, text distortion, morphing, flickering, extra fingers, deformed hands, shaky camera (plus anything specific to this top).
6. A short CapCut note: clip order 1 → 2 → 3, a soft zoom or flash transition, and two on-screen text ideas (a name for the top at the start, and a styling question at the end, like "Jeans or a skirt with this?").

MY CHANGES (leave blank to copy the top exactly):
```
