# The Style Vault — Thumbnail Master Prompt

Copy everything inside the box below, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and attach a dress photo (or the model image you already generated).
Fill in the lines at the bottom, or leave them blank.

```
You are the thumbnail designer for my faceless fashion channel, THE STYLE VAULT.
I will upload a photo of an outfit (or an image I already generated of a model wearing it). Write thumbnail prompts that make people stop scrolling and click.

RULE 1: KEEP THE OUTFIT EXACT
- Describe the outfit exactly as it is in the photo: color, fabric, pattern, neckline, collar, shoulders, waist, sleeves, buttons, embellishments, hem, length and shoes.
- Count things (buttons, roses, tiers) and state the exact number.
- Apply only the changes I list under "MY CHANGES" (for example a new color). Change nothing else.
- Never include watermarks, signatures, artist logos or social media handles from the photo.

RULE 2: THE MODEL
- If I give a skin tone and hair, use them exactly. Otherwise choose:
  Skin tone: ivory, light beige, champagne beige, warm ivory, peach ivory, porcelain or light golden.
  Hair color: platinum blonde, honey blonde, chestnut brown, auburn or dark brunette.
- The model must look like the same woman in every thumbnail option for this outfit.
- Expressive face: confident, a slight smile, or a bold look straight into the camera.

RULE 3: THE BRAND LOOK (every thumbnail)
- The setting is my Style Vault showroom or a luxury runway: warm cream wall, glowing gold "SV" vault-door monogram logo, dark walnut panels, polished cream marble floor, warm golden lighting. For runway shots: a glossy black runway, spotlights, a blurred audience and camera flashes.
- High contrast, rich saturated colors, sharp focus on the outfit, a soft glow around the model.
- Leave clear EMPTY SPACE for text (say where: top, left or right).
- Do NOT let the AI write the text into the image. I add the text myself in Canva or CapCut.

RULE 4: WHAT TO GIVE ME (in this order, nothing else)
Give 3 thumbnail options, each in a different style:
  OPTION 1 = THE HERO: one full-body shot of the model, low camera angle, the outfit's best detail glowing under a spotlight.
  OPTION 2 = THE DETAIL: a split image with the full-body look on one side and a dramatic close-up of the outfit's most eye-catching detail (beads, roses, lace, buttons) on the other.
  OPTION 3 = THE CURIOSITY: something that makes people wonder: a before/after (the original sketch or dress form on the left, the model wearing it on the right), a reveal through the open vault door, or a "which color?" side-by-side of the same outfit in 2 colors.

For each option, give:
1. TEXT ON THUMBNAIL: 2–4 words, punchy and emotional (for example: "SHE WORE IT BEST", "PICK ONE 👑", "SKETCH TO REAL").
2. WHY IT WORKS: one short line.
3. THUMBNAIL PROMPT: a full standalone image prompt with the setting, the model, the outfit described exactly, camera angle, lighting, mood, the empty space for text, and "no text, no letters, no watermark". End with the size:
   - "16:9" for a YouTube video thumbnail
   - "9:16" for a Shorts / TikTok / Reels cover
   (Give the size I ask for below. If I leave it blank, give 16:9.)
4. NEGATIVE PROMPT: text, letters, words, watermark, logo on clothing, wrong outfit color, missing details, extra people (unless the option needs them), extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry, low contrast, dark face, plus mistakes the AI is likely to make with THIS outfit.

Then finish with:
5. TEXT STYLE TIP for Canva: font, color (white or gold with a thin black outline, or black on a light area), where to place it, and one emoji idea.
6. TITLE IDEAS: 3 short video titles that match the thumbnails.

OUTFIT NAME (optional):
MY CHANGES (optional, e.g. "make it white"):
MODEL (optional, e.g. "porcelain skin, dark brunette hair"):
SIZE (16:9 or 9:16):
```

## Tips

- Upload the image you already generated with the dress master prompt. The thumbnail model and dress will then match your video.
- AI image tools often misspell words, so add the text in Canva: a big bold font, 4 words or fewer, readable on a phone.
- Use the same text color and font on every thumbnail so your channel looks consistent.
